Section 2
Visitor Flow
How a new visitor moves through the site — from first landing to buying from Brent's recommended vendor.
1

Land on the Site

Visitor arrives at brent.getbotpacks.com

They see a clear headline explaining what the site does, and a "How It Works" 3-step orientation — so they instantly understand this isn't a generic blog. It's a personalized consultation engine built on Brent's expertise.

2

Orient & Pick a Topic

Visitor reads the 3-step guide or taps a topic chip

Topic chips — Recovery · Stacking · Hormones · Nutrition — pre-fill the chat box with a starter question. Zero friction to get started.

Visitor taps "Recovery" → chat box fills: "What peptides support faster recovery after training?"
3

Type Their Situation

Visitor uses the single chat box to describe their actual situation

Not a form. Not a quiz. Just a conversational input — the way they'd text a friend who happened to be a peptide expert.

"I'm on metformin, doing intermittent fasting, and want to improve recovery after lifting. What's safe to stack?"
4

AI Connects the Dots

The AI reasons across Brent's full research library

It doesn't just search for keywords — it reasons: "metformin + IF affects mTOR... BPC-157 timing matters here... this stack has a conflict at this dose." It surfaces connections a visitor couldn't make themselves.

⚡ This is what makes it worth coming back to
5

Email Gate

First-time visitors without email on file

Anonymous visitors get a directional answer — enough to see the value — then a prompt to enter their email for the full consultation depth. Email on file = full access, always.

🔒 "You're on the right track — enter your email to unlock Brent's full protocol recommendation."
6

Recommendation + Affiliate CTA

Inside the AI answer — not a sidebar, not a banner

The answer includes Brent's specific vendor recommendation, woven naturally into the response. One yellow button to take action.

"Brent recommends BPC-157 from [Vendor Name] — third-party tested, the source he uses personally."
Get Brent's Recommended BPC-157 →

Visitor clicks → goes to vendor → Brent earns commission

7

Keep Exploring

Visitor stays engaged — and comes back

Visitor scrolls to the FAQ for quick common questions. Reads this week's blog post. Bookmarks the site. Returns next week when Brent publishes new research. The site gets more valuable over time — for visitors and for Brent.

📬 New blog post = reason to return = more affiliate clicks
Section 3
How Brent Maintains It
Everything Brent needs to do to keep the site fresh and growing. No tech skills required — all of it happens through Pal chat.
Step 1

Add Research

Brent finds a new study, article, or protocol. He pastes it or uploads it to his Pal chat. Done. The AI starts reasoning with it immediately.

"Pal, add this to my knowledge base"
→ Knowledge base updated ✓
Step 2

Update FAQ

Brent gets a question he wants to answer publicly. He tells Pal. Pal writes the answer in his voice and adds it to the FAQ section — live in minutes.

"Pal, add this to FAQ: What's the difference between BPC-157 and TB-500?"
→ FAQ updated ✓
Step 3

Publish Blog Post

Brent gives Pal a topic or drops in a study. Pal writes the full post in Brent's voice. Brent reads it, approves, and it publishes automatically.

"Pal, write this week's post on peptide timing around fasted training"
→ Draft ready for approval ✓
Step 4

AI Gets Smarter

Every document Brent adds expands what the AI can reason across. No re-training. No tech work. The consultation engine improves automatically over time.

More documents → deeper answers
→ Better recommendations → more commissions ✓
