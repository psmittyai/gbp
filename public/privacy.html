1. Overview

PAL (Persistent Asset Library) is a product of GetBotPacks, operated at getbotpacks.com. PAL is a personal AI assistant and knowledge library that helps you capture, organize, summarize, and query information from the web and your own content.

This Privacy Policy covers:

  • The PAL web application at [slug].getbotpacks.com
  • The PAL Chrome Extension available on the Chrome Web Store

We take your privacy seriously. PAL is built on a simple principle: your data is yours. We collect only what is necessary, transmit only what you explicitly request, and never sell your data to anyone.

The short version: PAL only reads or transmits webpage content when you press a button to take an action. There is no background recording, no passive browsing history collection, and no advertising. You control what gets saved.

2. What Data We Collect

2a. Account Data

When you create a PAL account, we collect:

  • Your chosen account slug (your personal subdomain, e.g. yourname.getbotpacks.com)
  • Your password (stored as a salted hash — never in plain text)
  • Optional: email address (only if you provide it for account recovery)

2b. Content You Save (Assets)

PAL stores the content you explicitly choose to save. This may include:

  • Page URLs and titles from websites you visit
  • Selected text snippets you highlight and save
  • Full page content (if you choose the "full page" capture mode)
  • Your own notes, project entries, and research items
  • AI-generated summaries and responses to your questions

This content is stored only when you take an explicit action — clicking Save, Summarize, Ask PAL, or Create Asset. PAL does not passively record your browsing.

2c. Usage Data

Like most web services, our servers may log standard technical information when you access PAL:

  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • Timestamps of requests
  • Error logs

This data is used for security, debugging, and service operation — not for advertising or user profiling.

2d. What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Browsing history (we do not record which sites you visit)
  • ❌ Keystrokes or form inputs on third-party websites
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Camera or microphone access
  • ❌ Personal financial information
  • ❌ Health or biometric data

4. AI Processing (Claude / Anthropic)

PAL uses Claude, the AI model developed by Anthropic, PBC, to power its AI features — including summaries, "Ask PAL" queries, and content analysis.

When you trigger an AI action (e.g. "Summarize this page" or "Ask PAL a question"), the relevant content (page text or your selected text) is sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic returns the AI-generated response, which PAL then displays and optionally stores in your library.

Key facts about AI data handling:

  • Content is sent to Anthropic only when you trigger an AI action — never passively
  • Anthropic processes content under its own Privacy Policy
  • PAL operates under Anthropic's API usage policy, which prohibits training on API-submitted content by default
  • We do not knowingly send sensitive personal information (passwords, financial data) through AI actions

By using PAL's AI features, you acknowledge that content you submit for processing is shared with Anthropic for the purpose of generating an AI response.

6. Data Storage & Retention

Where Data Is Stored

Your PAL data is stored on PAL servers hosted via Railway.app infrastructure. Data is associated with your account slug and protected by your password.

How Long We Retain Data

  • Account data & saved assets: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Individual assets: Deleted immediately when you delete them from your library
  • Full account deletion: All data permanently deleted when you delete your account
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 90 days for security purposes, then purged

Security

We protect your data using industry-standard practices: HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed password storage, and access controls. No security system is perfect — please choose a strong, unique password for your PAL account.

8. Chrome Extension — Specific Disclosures

The PAL Chrome Extension places a floating purple brain button on webpages you visit, allowing you to save, summarize, query, or send page content directly to your PAL account. This section provides specific disclosures required for Chrome Web Store compliance.

Permissions Used and Why

Permission Why It's Needed
<all_urls> (host access) Required to display the floating PAL button on every webpage and to read page content when you choose to save or process it. PAL does not continuously monitor or record your browsing activity.
storage (chrome.storage.local) Stores your account slug and authentication token locally on your device, so you stay logged in without re-entering credentials on every page.
Service Worker (background) Handles communication between the extension's content script and PAL servers. The service worker is not used for passive data collection.

What the Extension Reads and When

The extension only accesses webpage content when you take an action. Clicking the brain button opens a panel. Pressing an action button (Save, Summarize, Ask PAL, Create Asset) transmits the relevant content to PAL. Until you press an action button, no content is read or transmitted.

Data Transmitted by the Extension

When you trigger an action, the extension sends to PAL servers:

  • Current page URL and title
  • Page text content OR your selected text (depending on your chosen capture mode)
  • Your account slug (to route data to your account)
  • Your auth token (to authenticate the request)

Data NOT Transmitted by the Extension

  • ❌ Your browsing history or visited URLs (passively)
  • ❌ Keystrokes, passwords, or form inputs
  • ❌ Content from pages you did not explicitly choose to process
  • ❌ Cookies or session data from third-party websites

Local Data Storage

The extension stores the following in chrome.storage.local on your device only:

  • Your PAL account slug
  • Your PAL authentication token

This data remains on your device and is never sent anywhere except to PAL's own servers to authenticate your requests.

Incognito Mode

The PAL extension is not enabled in Incognito mode by default (v1). To protect your privacy, the extension does not operate in Incognito windows unless you explicitly enable it in Chrome's extension settings.

Uninstalling the Extension

Uninstalling the PAL extension removes all locally stored extension data (your slug and token) from your device. Your PAL account and saved assets remain on PAL servers and can be accessed via your browser at [slug].getbotpacks.com. To delete your account and all data, contact support@getbotpacks.com.
